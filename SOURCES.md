# Scientific references

Reviewed 2026-10-23. References support measurement principles. They do not
specify the exterior, dimensions, component positions or rated performance of
the illustrative instruments. No manufacturer links or manual scans are included.

| Reference | Principle used |
| --- | --- |
| [Automated specimen handling, research paper (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2007GC001856) | Automated indexing, vacuum pickup, specimen insertion and orientation. The two handler capacities and frame geometry here are teaching configurations. |
| [Foner (1959), Versatile and Sensitive Vibrating-Sample Magnetometer](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1716679) | Motion-dependent inductive detection, differential pickups and reference-based measurement. |
| [DC-SQUID operating principles, university institute](https://www.ims.kit.edu/english/2056.php) | Two Josephson junctions, resistive shunts, current bias and periodic flux-to-voltage response. |
| [Applied Superconductivity, university course](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-763-applied-superconductivity-fall-2005/) | Josephson effects, superconducting loops and SQUID theory. The viewer's feedback law and RF damping are explicitly idealized teaching models. |
| [Instrumentation Amplifiers, university course](https://ocw.tudelft.nl/course-lectures/instrumentation-amplifiers/) | Three-op-amp differential amplification. The assumed 25 kΩ feedback resistors and unity-gain output stage set the displayed gain relation. |
| [Phase-sensitive detection, university teaching experiment](https://courses.washington.edu/phys431/lock-in/lock-in.php) | Coherent demodulation, noise rejection and response-time tradeoffs. RMS conventions and filter equations are documented in `MODEL.md`. |

The ideal dipole, circular-loop and gradiometer calculations are derived in
`MODEL.md` and checked numerically. Generic enclosure, cryostat, electronics,
coil geometry and motion paths are original illustrative arrangements. No
reference is presented as validation of those dimensions or a commercial replica.
